Methodology

How I build.

Grounding complex models in deterministic reality.

01 / Strict Pipeline Integrity

Data leakage invalidates research. I implement cross-validation folds strictly after transforms, feature engineering, and scalers to protect real-world generalization.

02 / Interpretability First

A high metric score is useless if clinical experts cannot audit the logic. I integrate SHAP (Shapley Additive Explanations) into modeling workflows to guarantee transparency.

03 / Pragmatic Utility

Not every problem requires massive parameter scales. I prioritize fast, rule-based NLP and efficient gradient boosting when they deliver faster turnaround with lower latency.
